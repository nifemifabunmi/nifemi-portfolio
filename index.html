Pfizer externship, April 2026, verified by Extern

Making scanned pharmaceutical supplier documents searchable

Supplier documents arrive as scanned, multi-document PDFs, so finding one answer means reading pages by hand. I prototyped a pipeline that reads them, splits them into documents, and answers questions about their contents.

  • Compared Tesseract, EasyOCR, and PaddleOCR on a scanned supplier PDF and recommended the best fit for automation.
  • Extracted text and bounding boxes with PyMuPDF, then built page-level segmentation that uses a local LLM to find document boundaries.
  • Compared three embedding models in a RAG pipeline and chose the best one for supplier-document queries.
  • Shipped a Gradio chatbot and a Colab demo reporting recall, MRR, and response metrics.

A quantitative trading engine that models the costs real trades face

Most beginner backtests assume instant, free fills. I built a modular engine that simulates latency and slippage, so strategy results look closer to what live trading would produce.

  • Real-time market data ingestion and multi-asset portfolio monitoring.
  • Simulated live execution with latency and slippage modeling.
  • Backtested moving-average crossover and RSI strategies on historical data.
  • Computed Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, and annualized return to compare strategies on a risk-adjusted basis.
